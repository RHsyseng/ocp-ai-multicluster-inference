Welcome to this lab focused on AI multicluster inference using OpenShift AI, designed to enhance your understanding and practical skills.

The first part of this lab introduces the essential concepts for planning and executing an AI multicluster inference deployment on OpenShift. The second part involves practical scenario where you will configure the inference infrastructure to support distributed multicluster inference requests.

[#lab-aim]
== Who Is This Lab Aimed At?

Technical profiles working with OpenShift AI who want to:

- Operate an **inference cluster fleet** to offer shared inference services.
- Work with **service providers** (telcos, neoclouds, sovereign operators) offering **inference as a service**.

[#what-you-will-achieve]
== What You Will Achieve

By completing this lab you will be able to:

- Understand foundational multicluster AI inference concepts.
- Explore the OpenShift AI and Praxis AI Grid components involved.
- Deploy and expose generative AI models across clusters.
- Build an inference grid by onboarding inference clusters.
- Configure smart routing policies at the AI Gateway.
- Onboard tenants into the multicluster inference platform.
- Consume models through a single, stable endpoint from real workloads.
- Monitor usage, routing decisions, and platform health.
- Troubleshoot the deployment.

This comprehensive journey will equip you with the knowledge and practical skills necessary to operate an AI multicluster inference platform built on OpenShift AI.
