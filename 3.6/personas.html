Personas

Multicluster inference separates the administration of the serving-cluster fleet (the cloud provider) from the consumption of entitled models (the organizations). This section describes the personas that appear across that boundary.

Roles

Persona Who they are Primary responsibilities

Cloud Provider Admin

The infrastructure and platform operator

Owns the clusters, deploys models, sets routing policy, and defines subscription tiers. Onboards organizations and sees everything (GPU, token usage) across all organizations and locations.

Organization Admin

A tenant-scoped operator

Grants their users access to entitled models and reviews usage for their organization only. Connects the organization’s identity provider and manages its users and groups.

Organization User

A consumer within an organization

Self-service: creates API keys and subscriptions, runs inference, and sees their own usage. Never selects a cluster.
Billing is out of scope for this release. The usage visibility described above is token/usage metering only; integration with metering and billing systems is planned for future releases.
Bring your own model (BYOM) is on the roadmap, however it won’t be part of the RHOAI 3.6 release.

What an Organization Admin can and cannot do

The Organization Admin operates entirely within the boundary the cloud provider granted. This boundary is what makes delegation safe.

An Organization Admin can:

  • Review the organization’s provider-issued subscriptions, groups, models, and service-limit (token-limit) information.

  • Connect the organization’s identity provider and manage its users and groups (see Identity Management).

  • Grant users access to entitled models within the provider-issued boundary.

  • Review and revoke the organization’s user API keys for subscriptions they administer.

An Organization Admin cannot:

  • Change provider-issued subscription limits or routing policy.

  • View another organization’s models or usage data.

  • Deploy, copy, place, update, or delete models through the grid.
